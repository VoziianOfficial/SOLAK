Overview

Compare provider options for new solar panel projects.

A solar panel installation project can involve property review, roof conditions, energy usage goals, equipment options, permitting questions, warranty terms, and provider availability.

SOLAK helps users explore those considerations as a comparison platform. The goal is to make the quote path clearer before users decide which independent solar companies they want to contact.

Aggregator-safe note

SOLAK does not install solar panels directly and does not act as a contractor. Independent providers are responsible for their own quotes, terms, licenses, insurance, and project work.
