2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on a device by a website. They can help websites remember user preferences, recognize a returning browser, support security, and understand how visitors interact with pages.

Similar technologies may include local storage, session storage, tracking pixels, analytics scripts, and browser identifiers.

SOLAK may use different categories of cookies and similar technologies depending on site functionality, analytics setup, and user preferences.

Cookie type Purpose Examples

Performance cookies Help understand how users interact with the website and identify usability issues. Page visits, general usage patterns, device or browser type.
Functional cookies Remember user choices and improve convenience across pages. Saved preferences, form interaction improvements, display choices.
Analytics technologies Measure website traffic, content performance, and page interaction trends. Aggregated analytics reports, page engagement, referral sources.
Marketing or referral cookies May help understand campaign performance or provider request sources where applicable. Campaign tags, referral tracking, ad-related measurement.
