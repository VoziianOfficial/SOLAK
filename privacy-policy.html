SOLAK Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how SOLAK handles information related to users who visit the website, submit a form, request provider options, or contact the platform.

Last updated:

1. Overview

SOLAK is an independent solar provider comparison platform. The website helps users explore solar service categories and request quote options from independent providers. SOLAK is not a solar contractor and does not perform solar services directly.

By using this website, you acknowledge that information you submit may be used to respond to your request, provide platform support, improve the website, and help facilitate relevant provider comparison opportunities.

Important

Users should verify licensing, insurance, scope, warranties, pricing, and terms directly with any independent provider before hiring.

2. Information We May Collect

SOLAK may collect information that users voluntarily submit through website forms, email, phone contact, or other platform interactions.

  • Name and contact information, such as email address and phone number.
  • Location information, such as ZIP code or general service area.
  • Solar interest category, such as residential solar, commercial solar, battery storage, maintenance, or consultation.
  • Message content submitted through contact or quote request forms.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device type, pages viewed, and approximate interaction data.

3. How We May Use Information

Information collected through the website may be used for practical platform purposes, including responding to user requests and improving the user experience.

  • To respond to solar provider comparison requests.
  • To communicate with users about submitted inquiries.
  • To organize requests by solar service category and service area.
  • To improve website performance, usability, content, and security.
  • To maintain internal records related to inquiries and support communication.
  • To comply with applicable legal, safety, or operational requirements.

4. Information Sharing

SOLAK may share submitted request information with independent solar providers, service partners, or operational vendors when reasonably necessary to support a user’s request or operate the website.

Independent providers are separate businesses and are responsible for their own quotes, communications, privacy practices, services, licenses, insurance, warranties, and project terms.

  • We may share inquiry details to help facilitate provider comparison opportunities.
  • We may use trusted vendors for hosting, analytics, form processing, security, or communication support.
  • We may disclose information when required by law or to protect website security and platform rights.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

SOLAK may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to remember cookie preferences, improve functionality, measure website performance, and understand general usage patterns.

For more details, please review the Cookie Policy.

6. Data Security

SOLAK uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information submitted through the website. However, no website, internet transmission, or digital storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should avoid submitting sensitive personal, financial, medical, or confidential information through general website forms unless specifically requested through a secure and appropriate channel.

7. Data Retention

SOLAK may retain submitted information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, operate the platform, maintain records, resolve disputes, improve the website, and comply with legal or operational obligations.

8. User Choices

Users may contact SOLAK to request updates, corrections, or deletion of certain information where applicable and reasonable. Some information may be retained when necessary for legal, security, operational, or recordkeeping purposes.

  • You may choose not to submit a website form.
  • You may contact SOLAK by email for privacy-related requests.
  • You may manage cookie preferences through browser settings or the cookie banner when available.

9. Third-Party Links and Providers

The website may reference or connect users with independent providers or third-party services. SOLAK is not responsible for third-party websites, provider practices, external privacy policies, or independent provider communications.

Users should review any provider’s own privacy policy, terms, licensing, insurance, and service documentation before sharing additional information or hiring.

10. Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for adults seeking solar provider comparison information. SOLAK does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

SOLAK may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates may be posted on this page with a revised date. Continued use of the website after updates means you accept the revised policy.
