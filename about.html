About SOLAK

Company story

Solar decisions can feel complex. SOLAK makes comparison easier.

Solar projects can vary by property type, roof condition, energy goals, equipment preferences, battery needs, provider coverage, warranty terms, financing discussions, and quote details.

SOLAK was built to help users organize those decisions into clear solar categories. Instead of presenting one direct service offer, SOLAK helps homeowners and businesses explore provider options and request quote paths from independent solar companies.

Independent comparison platform

SOLAK does not perform solar installation, repair, maintenance, or consultation services directly. Users should verify license, insurance, scope, and terms with any independent provider before hiring.
